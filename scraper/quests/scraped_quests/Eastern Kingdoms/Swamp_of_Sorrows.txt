The direct route from Swamp of Sorrows to Badlands is through Alliance territory (on non PvP servers theres still guards to deal with) and 50+ zones.&nbsp The simple way for Horde is to start out in Undercity. Walk/fly to Hammerfall (pick up the Sep and TM FPs if you don't have them), from Hammerfall go south into Wetlands, south again until you find Dun Algaz, into and through Loch Modan until you get near Thelsamar, skirt around Thelsamar on the lake side and when you get to the southern tip of the Loch (lake) go south into Badlands. Faile's suggestion of coming through Gnomeregan/Dun Morogh also works (great idea!) because the guards you will meet are low level and easily avoidable, especially if you already know the dwarf/gnome starter area. From the Loch Modan/Badlands border go south past the Uldaman meeting stone then stay within sight of the western mountains and follow them all the way to Kargath.&nbsp The trick to making this work is to avoid getting too close to Angor Fortress, being prepared for the stealthed cats to jump you between Angor and Lotwil's camp and avoiding the rock elementals between there and Kargath. The Kargath FP is also the keystone FP that will connect all your UC area FPs to the Stonard/Grom'gol/BB FPs.
