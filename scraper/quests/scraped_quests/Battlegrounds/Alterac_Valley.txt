The rep reward for that is 10 rep with Orgrimmar per 20 [url='http://classicdb.ch/?item=17422']armor scraps[/url] turned in. This might not seem like a lot of rep at first, but keep in mind that armor scraps are some of the most abundant drops off of your fallen enemies, often dropping ~20 armor scraps apiece. If you are constantly in the heat of battle and remember to loot the ones who are killed, you can quite easily pick up several hundred armor scraps to turn in.
