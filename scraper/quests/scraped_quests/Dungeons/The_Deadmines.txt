I'll lay out some working strategies as best I can. In the corridors, which is most of the zone, you just need to watch what/how you pull. I've pulled the entire dungeon as a level 22 Hunter and never gotten more than one add. You really need someone with a ranged attack to pull. If it's a caster who shouldn't be tanking, they need to use their lowest level spell to pull with, so they don't cause much initial aggro. If it HAS to be a paladin or druid, then they need to pull the mob and back up quite a bit. Runners in here will get you adds, which cause wipes. The first boss is an ogre. He walks up and down a path. If you've never done this before, watch him a couple times, and pull him when he's farthest from the door to avoid adds. There is a patrol after him, which will need to be neutralized before going through teh door. The next boss is the Shredder, and it's pilot (Sneed?). This is fairly simple, as the shredder also has a set path. Pull him just as he begins walking up the hill, but make sure all the goblins around the wood are dead. The second and final patrol is here. Next, you go down another corridor and into the Foundry. This is where most groups wipe. It is IMPERATIVE that your puller pay attention to the pathing of the goblins. Often they won't follow, but instead they will begin shooting. You will have to back out of their gun range to force them away from their friends to prevent adds. A single add in this area is difficult for a low level group, and can cause a wipe quite quickly. Also, the goblins in this area seem to have a high aggro range. Gilnik? is the boss of this area, over next to the door. He's not really too tough. Next up is another corridor which ends with a cannon pointed at the door. You'll need blasting powder from a room nearby to activate the cannon. This leads to the docks. The docks are easy until you get to the ramp leading up to the ship. There will be 2 stealthed rogues at the bottom of the ramp, and Smite, the Tauren, at the top. The main tank should engage Smite at the wide area before the turn to the plank, while the rest of the group deals with the rogues even further back. The healer should be at maximum range from the warrior to avoid Smite's stun. The reason for being farther away from the ramp is so that Smite will have to cover distance to run to his chest to switch his weapons, allowing more time for the stun to wear off. Once the rogues are dealt with, the rest of the group can engage Smite. At the top of the ramp, going right will lead to Captain Greenskin and Van Cleef. Going left will lead to Cookie, the murlock cook, and the exit. Just keep pulling slowly and keep the mobs being engaged far enough away from any potential adds. Once you reach the top platform, watch out for Greenskin. He patrols the top deck where VC is, but he will assist mobs on the platform below, so wait on him to patrol around to the other side before pulling. Once everything else is dead, pull Greenskin when he reaches the plank which leads to the top deck. He usually has 2 or 3 mobs linked to him, so this fight can be rough. Once Greenskin is down, it's time for VC. VC has 2 rogue friends who will assist him in the fight. When he reaches half life, 2 more will spawn. Use the same strategy as with Smite, with the MT on VC while the rest of the group deals with the adds. VC doesn't have an AOE stun like Smite though, but he's tougher and has more DPS, so the healer will likely need to keep attention on the MT. As a 22 Hunter, I was able to use my pet to tank VC while I healed it, while the rest of the group dealt with the adds. This helped the healer both on mana and on aggro, though in the end the pet finally died when I ran OOM, and VC proceeded to kill me. The group succeeded in finishing him off, and a rez later and we headed for the zone. Experience really helps a lot here. Once you reach the docks, if a wipe seems imminent, jump in the water, as the pirates can't swim. Ironic, ain't it?
