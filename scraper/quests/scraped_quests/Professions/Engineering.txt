I don't remember how it was for gnomish, but anyway, let's clear something about the actual difference. To say it succintly, goblin engineering is: bombs, bombs and more bombs. You have all sorts of big bombs that makes things blow up. One particular bomb is above all: The Big One. It has a huge blasting radius (10 meters) and it will stun EVERYONE inside it, and it seems it cannot be resisted. This thing comes with a price though, unless you know an alchemist who can make you some goblin rocket fuel, making this will be quite expensive. There's also the Goblin Rocket Helm, my favourite! I'm a hunter, so sometimes I get stuck in melee combat, especially in PvP. But, after I got this, the little coward thieves who call themselves 'rogues' are very easy to beat. Just use this to a rogue, and the rogue feels how is it to get sapped! Just beware of doing any damage. Gnomish engineering. Lots and lots of funny weird gizmos that are effective yet unreliable. For example, there's this Gnomish Death Ray. Sure, it can crit 4000... but, what about when it does that to YOU? And, with the gnomish shrink ray, there's two risks: a) it might ENLARGE instead of shrinking b) it might shrink you and your PARTY. But, if you're brave enough and willing to take these risks, go on! I'm sure it will be great fun. I personally prefer goblin engineering, because I&nbsp 'm always after 100% reliance.
