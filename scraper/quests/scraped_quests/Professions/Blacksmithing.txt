However, bear in mind that your skills will be rising when you craft some of the items involved in this quest. (2) Talk to Expert Blacksmith Trainer in Ironforge at 210 skill in blacksmith. (3) He will direct you to Grumnus Steelshaper standing by the Great Forge in Ironforge who gives you a quest to make the following items: (a) Ornate Mithril Helmets X 4 (16 Mithril Bars X 4, 2 Truesilver Bar x 4, Solid Grinding Stone X 4, 4 Black Pearls X 4) (b) Ornate Mithril Boots X 2 (14 Mithril Bars X 2, 2 Truesilver Bar x 2, 4 Thick Leather x 2, Solid Grinding Stone x 2, Aquamarine X 2) (c) Ornate Mithril Breast Plate X 1 (16 Mithril Bars, 6 Truesilver, Heart of Fire, Solid Grinding Stone) At which point you will be stumped as you neither have the recipes/know how to craft them nor do you know where to head to...till Anonymous's guides came along. He will give you a quest called 'The Origins of Smithing' which requires you to craft 6 Golden Scale Bracers [30 Steel Bars, 12 Heavy Grinding Stones (translates to 36 Heavy Stones)]. Once completed, he will give you a quest to see another NPC by the name of McGavan in Booty Bay. (5) Talk to McGavan who then gives you a quest called 'The Mithril Order' to talk to Galvan the Ancient. ('Galvan') Galvan is between Zul Gurub and the Mosh'Ogg Ogre mound in STV. (230 blacksmith skill pre-requisite) (6) Once you meet Galvan, he will give you 3 quests: (a) Bring 40 Mithril Bars, 5 True Silver to him. ('The Great Silver Deceiver') (b) Bring 40 Mithril Bars, 40 Iron Bars to him. ('Smelt On, Smelt Off') (c) Bring 40 Mithril Bars, 4 Citrine to him. ('The Art of Imbue') Completing each quest will allow you to learn more recipes needed to complete your ultimate quest. Once all quests are done, speak to Galvan again and he will then direct you to see another NPC by the name of Trenton Lighthammer in the town of Gadgetzan in Tanaris. ('Galvan's Finest Pupil') Trenton is by a forge in the town of Gadgetzan. (12 Mithril Bars, 1 Truesilver, 1 Solid Grinding Stone, 1 Aquamarine) (c) The Mithril Kid which requires crafting Heavy Mithril Breastplate X 2 (16 Mithril Bars X 2), Ornate Mithril Gloves X 1.
