In a nutshell, both the Horde and the Alliance banded together to gather supplies for the war effort, which included thousands of stacks of cloth, leather, metal bars, bandages, herbs, meat and fish in order to fuel a war effort against the Ahn'Qiraj race in Silithus (the controllers of the Silithid bugs).
